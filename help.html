Help

The Install button doesn't appear

Installing needs Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS computer. Firefox, Safari and phones can't do it.

My board isn't in the list

  • Try another USB cable: many cables only charge.
  • The board may be asleep, and then its USB port is off. Hold the BOOT button while you plug it in, let go, and press Install again.
  • Close anything else using the board, such as the Arduino IDE's serial monitor.

It finished, but the screen is dark

Unplug the board and plug it back in. These boards don't always start by themselves after being installed.

Which board do I have?

The name is printed on the back of the board. The 1.75C is the one with a small speaker socket and two microphone holes.

Something went wrong partway

Nothing is broken: hold BOOT, plug it in, and install again.
